In the previous laboratory exercise, your group developed a website presenting your proposed

schedule is not enough. The project needs a properly organized team.
For this laboratory exercise, your group will transform itself into a formal project team. Each member
must be assigned a role based on their skills, strengths, and expected contribution to the proposed
system.
